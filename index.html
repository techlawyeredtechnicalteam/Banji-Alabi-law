        A full-service firm with a strong hand in property.

        Banji Alabi & Co advises individuals, families and businesses from its head office in Lekki, Lagos, with real estate, revenue and tax, and oil and gas advisory at the centre of its practice.

        Banji Alabi, Esq. is a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria and the principal partner of Banji Alabi & Co. The firm has a special bias for real estate, revenue generation and tax consultancy, and oil and gas advisory, and it has developed estates as well as advising on them.

        Before founding the firm he spent 24 years at Chevron Nigeria Limited, from 1990 to 2014. That period gave him corporate discipline, commercial expertise in the oil sector and strategic training that became the base for his private practice.

        He was the first alumnus appointed Chairman of the Governing Council of Rufus Giwa Polytechnic, Owo, and the first to be reappointed for a second term. His submission to the National Conference argued for true federalism and resource control.

        The firm was established with other seasoned professionals, including his wife and co-principal, Mrs Omolola Banji-Alabi, a Chartered Banker and former staff of Union Homes and Oceanic Bank.

          Property costs and conveyancing fees are separate

          The cost of a property transaction, including the purchase price and any charges, taxes or fees payable on the property itself, is distinct from our conveyancing fees.

          Our conveyancing fees cover only the legal work we do for you. We confirm them in writing before we begin, so you know what you are paying for and to whom.
